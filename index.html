From Disease to Target to Compound.

Explore disease-associated targets and prioritize compound candidates using biomedical databases and a Greedy Best-First Search over the resulting knowledge graph.

Open Targets+ ChEMBL+ Greedy Best-First Search+ Heuristic Ranking

Rankings are heuristic prioritizations derived from database evidence and user-selected parameters. They are not clinical efficacy claims and do not indicate a "best drug" or "recommended treatment".

"Run Exploration" calls Open Targets and ChEMBL live. "Run Demo Example" replays a recorded Alzheimer's-disease snapshot through the same Greedy Best-First Search engine — useful when you are offline or want a guaranteed-fast walkthrough. Demo results are always labeled DEMO and are never shown as if they were live data.
